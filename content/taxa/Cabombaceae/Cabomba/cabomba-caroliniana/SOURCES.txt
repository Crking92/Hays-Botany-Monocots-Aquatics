MORPHOLOGY
FNA: opposite fan-shaped finely divided submerged leaves; floating leaves near flowering; solitary 3-merous flowers; petals with yellow nectar-bearing auricles.
Austin wetland guide gives the same local morphology and Texas-native treatment.
